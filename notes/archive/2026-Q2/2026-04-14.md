## Hallway track

Tags: #talk-idea #migration

Three different people came up after the session with the same problem: how do you rehearse a database move when you can't get your hands on production data? Legal won't sign off on cloning customer rows, and staging is two major versions behind, so every dry run lies to them.

What they do today:

- restore an old backup onto a laptop and hope the schema hasn't drifted
- run the cutover script against empty tables and call it tested
- skip the rehearsal → book a long maintenance window instead

There's a talk in this, maybe a lab. Build the rehearsal environment from the schema plus generated rows that match the real shape of the data -- row counts, NULL rates, the long tail of text lengths. Load it, run the move, compare before and after. You don't need one real customer row to find out the cutover breaks on a NULL.

The person from the logistics company offered row counts per table, no data. Follow up next week.
