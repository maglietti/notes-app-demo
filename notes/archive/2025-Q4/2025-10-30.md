## Backup docs gap

Tags: #docs #backup

Three separate threads this month asked the same thing: the backup docs stop at a full backup. Nobody explains incremental backups, how to chain them, or what to do when a restore fails halfway through.

Outline for a tutorial:

- full backup, then two incrementals
- restore the chain onto a fresh server
- break it on purpose -- delete one incremental -- and show the error
- what to check before you trust a restore
