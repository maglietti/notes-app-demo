## Contributor program idea

Tags: #community

New contributors bounce off because they can't find a first task that's small enough. Idea: a curated list of ten starter issues, each with enough context that someone can start without a meeting.

Pair each newcomer with a maintainer for their first contribution. Docs examples are the easiest win -- low risk, high value, and they teach the codebase.
